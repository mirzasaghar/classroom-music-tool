Recorded instrument sounds used by the music tool

Grand piano (sounds/piano): Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, recorded from a Yamaha C5.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3 (MP3 edition by Jan Forst, npm @audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity8)

Organ (sounds/organ) and flute (sounds/flute): Fluid R3 General MIDI SoundFont by Frank Wen, rendered by
Benjamin Gleitzman (github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts). Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Changes: every third note from C2 to C7 kept, converted to mono MP3; piano notes cut to five seconds with a
one-second fade at the end.

Drum kit (sounds/drums): Virtuosity Drums by Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples, recorded
with close, overhead and room microphones. Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication),
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums

Changes to the drums: the close, overhead and room microphones mixed to one stereo file per hit (room at
about a third), up to four velocity layers and two alternate takes per piece, cut to length with a fade,
converted to MP3. The level of each piece is set in drums/gains.json.
